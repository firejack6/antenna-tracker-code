Every interval over 100 ms between valid ADC batches counts once. Ongoing outages are logged before recovery. Monitoring continues with the browser closed and CSV recording off; the PC and relay must stay awake. Counts reset on a new Connect; previous logs remain on disk.

Sequence gaps include missed ADC deadlines and discarded data; they do not measure Wi-Fi packet loss. Buffer delay is estimated from queued scans and the reported acquisition rate. TCP stalls count writes that waited for socket space.
